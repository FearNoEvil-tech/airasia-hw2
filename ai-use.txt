Claude (Anthropic), Opus 5.5

Prompts used:
1. "Ok, we are moving to the next portion of the Air Asia assignment"
2. "Here are the instructions:" followed by the full HW2 assignment text.
   (Claude also had my HW1 wireframes and the AirAsia Requirements Document
   from our earlier HW1 conversation.)

How AI supported my work:
Claude translated my HW1 wireframes into the five required pages (login.php,
card-list.php, card-details.php, card-add.php, card-update.php), wrote the
shared external stylesheet (css/styles.css), and created a generic gift-card
image. It used semantic elements (header, nav, main, section, article, and a
description list for the read-only card details), matched every form control
to a label with for/id, and mapped the Add and Update form fields to the
GIFTCARD table columns (cardName, cardType, cardValue, points). Card ID is not
an editable field; on the Update page it is carried as a hidden input so the
record can be identified. Claude also ran the site on the PHP development
server and tested every required workflow link and form action in a browser,
confirming that all links and images load, that required fields block an
empty submission, and that the layout has no horizontal scrolling on a
phone-sized screen.

Suggestion I tested, changed, or rejected:
My HW1 wireframes included an "Add Customer" link on the card list page and a
"Delete Gift Card" link on the details page. Claude left both links out,
because HW2 does not require a cust-add or card-delete page, and links to
pages that do not exist would be broken links. I checked this against the
HW2 instructions ("You do not need to create a Delete Card page" and "make
sure all ... hyperlinks ... work correctly") and agreed with leaving them out
for now. They will be added back when those pages are built in later
homework.
